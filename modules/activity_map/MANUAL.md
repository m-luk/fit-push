# All activities map

## What it does

Shows the routes of all your activities with GPS on one large map. You can narrow
it down by sport, by tag and by date, and colour the routes either by sport (to see
where you ride versus run) or by date (to see where you trained recently versus
long ago). Clicking a route opens that activity.

## How to use

1. Click **🗺️ Map** in the navigation bar at the top (next to Activities and Segments).
2. All routes appear, zoomed to fit. Hover a route to see its name, date and
   distance; it is highlighted. Click it to open the activity.
3. **Sports**: click one or more sport chips to show only those sports. Click a
   chip again to remove it; with no chip pressed, all sports are shown. Each chip's
   colour square is the colour of that sport's routes.
4. **Tags**: works the same way with your activity tags (add tags on an activity
   page under its title). Only shown when you have tags.
5. **Dates**: pick a preset (*Last 30 days*, *This year*, …) or set **from** /
   **to** dates for a custom range.
6. **Colour by**: *Sport* uses one colour per sport (legend bottom-left);
   *Date* goes from light blue (oldest shown) to dark blue (newest), with the date
   span in the legend.
7. Big round markers show where activities start; a number means several start close
   together. Click a marker to zoom in and fit those activities. Once a route is big
   enough to see, its marker disappears.
8. The filters are part of the page address, so you can bookmark a view (for
   example "gravel rides this year").

## Limitations

- Only activities with GPS are shown (indoor and pool sessions are skipped).
- Routes are simplified to about one point every 25 m for speed; open the activity
  for the full-resolution track.
- Up to 8 sports get their own colour; any further sports share a gray "Other".
- At most 2000 routes are drawn at once; narrow the filters if you have more.
- The map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, so viewing the map shares the map area
  with their tile servers, as on any activity page.
