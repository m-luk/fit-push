# Gear

## What it does

Keeps track of your equipment (shoes, bikes, skis, boats and anything else) and how
much you have used it: total distance, moving time, number of activities and the last
time you used it. Give an item a distance limit and a bar shows when it is due for
replacement. Pick a default gear for a sport (e.g. Running) or for a whole group of
sports (e.g. All cycling), and every new activity of that sport gets it automatically.

## How to use

1. Open **Gear** in the navigation bar at the top and click **Add gear**.
2. Enter a **Name**, choose the **Kind**, and optionally a **Starting distance** (what
   the item had before you started tracking it) and **Replace after** (the distance
   limit). Add **Notes** if you like.
3. Under **Default for**, tick the sports or sport groups (**All cycling**, **All
   running**, …) that should use this item, then click **Save**. Each sport or group has
   one default per kind: ticking one that another item of the same kind holds (shown as
   "now …") moves it to this item. A sport's own default beats its group's, so you can
   set a road bike for **All cycling** and an MTB for **Mountain biking**.
4. Activities you imported before setting defaults have no gear: click **Apply
   defaults** in the notice at the top of the **Gear** page.
5. To give gear to many activities at once, click **No gear (N)** at the top of the
   **Gear** page. It lists every activity without gear; narrow it with the sport
   filter, tick activities (click a row, **Shift**+click for a range, or the box in the
   header for all shown), pick an item and click **Add to selected**. Click **Gear** to
   go back to your items.
6. On an activity page, the **Gear** card shows its gear. Click **Change**, tick the
   items you used and click **Save**. **Reset to defaults** undoes a manual choice.
7. When an item is worn out, click **Retire**: it keeps its history but is no longer
   assigned automatically. **Unretire** brings it back; **Delete** (click twice) removes
   it and its history.

## Limitations

- Defaults apply to new imports and to sport changes; changing a default never rewrites
  activities that already have gear. A gear choice made by hand is kept when you change
  the activity's sport.
- Distance and time come from the activity summary, so indoor activities without
  distance add only time.
- Trimmed copies (clips) get the default gear of their sport, not the gear of the
  original activity.
